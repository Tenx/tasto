PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TASTO ("TASTO", "we", "us", or "our"), operator of www.tasto.world (the "Site"), collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit the Site or purchase our product. By using the Site you agree to the practices described here.

01What we collect

We aim to collect as little personal data as possible. Depending on how you use the Site, we may process:

  • Purchase & billing information — when you buy lifetime access, payment is processed by our payment provider (see "Payments" below). We receive confirmation of a completed purchase and the associated email; we do not store your full card number or CVV.
  • Contact information — the email address you use to contact support or complete a purchase.
  • Usage & device data — standard technical data such as browser type, device, referring page, and interactions with the Site, collected to keep the Site secure and improve it.
  • Local storage — we store an unlock flag in your browser's local storage so the Site remembers that you have purchased access. This never leaves your device unless required to verify a purchase.

02How we use it

  • To deliver the product you purchased (unlocking all style prompts).
  • To provide customer support and respond to your enquiries.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Site.
  • To comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

03Payments

Payments are processed by Creem (Armitage Labs OÜ), acting as our Merchant of Record. When you make a purchase, your payment details are collected and processed directly by the payment provider under their own terms and privacy policy. We never see or store your full payment-card details. Please review the provider's policy at creem.io.

04Cookies & local storage

The Site uses browser local storage to remember your purchase state and essential functional preferences. We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate usage. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings; doing so may require you to re-verify your purchase.

05Sharing & third parties

We share information only with service providers who help us run the Site — for example our payment processor, hosting provider, and email/support tools — and only to the extent needed to provide the service. These providers are bound to protect your data. We may also disclose information where required by law.

06Data retention

We keep purchase and billing records for as long as necessary to fulfil the purchase, provide support, and meet legal and tax requirements. We retain other data only as long as it serves the purposes described above.

07Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, or to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@tasto.world and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

08Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

09Children

The Site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11Contact
